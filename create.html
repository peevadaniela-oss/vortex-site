Prices

One price for each shape and size. Your colours and words are included at no extra cost.

What goes into the price

  • Plastic for your print, including the odd print that has to be redone
  • Printer time: bigger prints take longer, from about 25 minutes to 4½ hours
  • Packing so it arrives safely
  • Hand checking of every print before it ships
