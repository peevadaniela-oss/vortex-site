Websites and tools that run the shop

  • Wix hosts the website, runs the store, the cart and checkout, and stores our photos.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts Baloo 2 and Nunito, both under the SIL Open Font License.
  • three.js (MIT licence) draws the 3D previews in Design your own and Create your own, including its Helvetiker font for the words on your print. It loads from jsDelivr.
  • Meshy makes the 3D models in "Invent something new with AI", through Cloudflare Workers.
  • GitHub keeps the website's code.
  • Bambu Lab runs MakerWorld, where we find designs to print.

Our own work

The photos of name letters, desk organizers and the dragon egg are prints we made ourselves. The infinity cube pictures, the shop drawings and the page backgrounds were made for Vortex Toys.
